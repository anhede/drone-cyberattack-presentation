Attacks

Bias Attacks

Adds constant bias $\,b\,$

$$ \begin{align*} \tilde{x}&=x+b \end{align*} $$

Noise Attacks

Adds white-noise $\,v_k \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$

$$ \begin{align*} \tilde{x}_k &= x_k+v_k \end{align*} $$

Delay Attacks

Delays the signal by $\,\tau\,$ steps

$$ \begin{align*} \tilde{x}_k = x_{k-\tau} \\ \end{align*} $$

Working Attacks

Delay

Compare attack time vs optimal time

$$ \begin{align*} \text{Delay} &= t_{attack} - t_{optimal} \\ \end{align*} $$

Deviation

Compare attack route vs optimal route

$$ \begin{align*} &\text{Deviation} = \sqrt{ \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^N (\min_{j} \left\| \vec{p}_k - \vec{p}^*_j \right\|_2)^2 } \\ \approx &\;\text{Average distance to closest point on optimal path} \\ \end{align*} $$
