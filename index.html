Implementation Roadmap

Week 1-2: Backup provider + DDoS protection
→ Existential threat eliminated

Week 2-3: Disable USB ports on playtest machines

Week 3-6: MFA rollout + Network segmentation

Week 6-12: Migrate to dedicated source control + Encrypted drives

Key Takeaways

1. One vulnerability dominates: Backup deletion accounts for 79% of total risk (9.75M€)

2. Hacktivists are the primary threat: Quite low capability, but zero concern for collateral damage

3. Cost-effective solutions exist: 51,000€ investment yields 86% risk reduction
